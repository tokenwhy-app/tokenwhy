# tokenwhy privacy policy

tokenwhy runs on your computer. It reads the log files that your AI coding agents
(Claude Code, Codex, opencode, Antigravity) write on your disk, and stores its own
statistics in a local cache.

tokenwhy does not collect, send or sell any personal data. It has no account, no
analytics and no network connection to our servers.

Links in the app (sources, credits) open in your web browser only when you click them.
